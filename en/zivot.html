My Journey

Life and Career Milestones

Overview of education, industrial engineering experience, forensic engineering, and current work in education and modern technologies.

1983 – 1987

Secondary Vocational School of Mechanical Engineering (Hungarian as Language of Instruction)

Mechanical engineering studies at Ždanovova 1, Košice, completed with honors during the period when final exams covered five subjects.

Graduation with Honors (5 subjects)
1987 – 1992

Technical University of Košice (TUKE)

Studies at the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering, specializing in Transport and Handling Machinery. Concurrent industrial design studies and completion of supplementary pedagogical qualification (teaching minimum).

Ing. Degree & Pedagogical Qualification
1989

Driving Course — Categories A, B, C, T

Completion of driving course and qualification for categories A, B, C, and T, issued by the Traffic Inspectorate of the Police Force District Directorate in Košice.

Driving License A, B, C, T
1993 – 1996

Early Experience and IT

11 months of active service as an officer candidate (ordnance technician) at the air base in Prešov. Followed by roles in commercial and IT companies (Antre, Treadstone, Kerko) focused on IT hardware service, software, and sales support.

Commercial & IT Experience
1995 – 2004

Court-Appointed Expert in Mechanical Engineering Structures

Specialized studies at the Institute of Forensic Engineering in Žilina. Official registration in the Ministry of Justice of the Slovak Republic register focusing on machinery, mechanical structures, and technical equipment assessment.

Forensic Expert (MS SR)
1997 – 2020

Information Architect at U.S. Steel Košice

Over 20 years in industrial IT. Building data warehouses (Oracle Datawarehouse, ExpressBasic), administering SCADA systems on Hot and Cold Strip Mills, process control for finishing lines, network integrations, architecture design, and development in C++, .NET, and ASP for production optimization.

20+ Years in Industrial IT
2021 – 2024

Informatics Teacher and ICT Coordinator

Passing practical experience on to younger generations. Teaching informatics and physics and serving as school digital coordinator at Private Grammar School FUTURUM, SZŠ Kechnec, and ZŠ L. Novomeského, along with certifications including CCNA 1 and Microsoft Azure.

Digital Coordination & IT Certifications
2024 – Present

SSOŠ ekonomicko-technická, Košice

Teaching informatics, programming, robotics, and web technologies (Postupimská 37, Košice), developing student hardware and microcontroller projects (micro:bit, Python, 3D printing), alongside managing my personal website, viticulture in Turňa nad Bodvou, and sports.

Informatics Teacher & Web Builder
