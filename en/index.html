About Me

I am based in Košice, working at the intersection of engineering, education, and honest hands-on craftsmanship. Following my mechanical engineering studies at TUKE and over twenty years of experience as an industrial information architect – where I designed databases, data warehouses, and process control systems for manufacturing lines – I now teach applied computer science, programming, robotics, and web technologies. My goal is to pass on practical thinking to students and show them how things truly work beneath the surface.

I apply the same ground-up approach outside of school as well, whether in winemaking, farming, or active sports – from endurance running, hiking, and marksmanship to competitive winter swimming. I believe in self-reliance, practicality, and meaningful work that leaves behind tangible results.
